Refunds

All payments are processed by Google Play, and refunds are handled by Google Play's own system:

  • Within 48 hours of purchase, you can usually get an automatic refund yourself from Google Play → Order history → find the order → "Report a problem".
  • After that window, or if self-service refund isn't offered, email support@lanternhold.app describing the issue. We respond within 3 business days and, where appropriate, will ask Google to process a refund on your behalf.
  • Google Play's own buyer-protection channel is always available to you directly from your purchase history, independent of us.

Support / complaints procedure

  1. Email support@lanternhold.app with a short description and, if relevant, the Google Play order number from your purchase confirmation.
  2. We aim to respond within 3 business days.
  3. For purchase/refund issues specifically, you can also go directly to Google Play → Order history → "Report a problem" at any time — that channel works independently of us.

The address above is kept in sync with the in-app "support_email" value (Firebase Remote Config) — both can be updated at any time without a Play Store release.
