What this site is

These pages are the canonical, independently-editable copies of the legal and billing disclosures shown inside the Scorewright app (Settings → Legal & billing info). They are hosted here — separately from the app's source code — so they can be corrected or updated at any time without a Play Store release. The current support contact email is managed via Firebase Remote Config inside the app; if it changes, this site is updated to match in the same commit.
