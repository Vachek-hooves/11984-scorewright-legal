What the app stores

All game data you create — saved games, player names, scores, rules notes, and match history — is stored only on your device (local database). It is never uploaded to us or any third party, and there is no account or sign-in.

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob shows banner ads to users on the free tier. AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and other data to serve and measure ads, per Google's AdMob privacy policy.
  • Google Play Billing processes the optional one-time "Pro" purchase. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; this app never sees your payment information.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collect anonymous usage events (e.g. which screens are opened, whether the Pro purchase completed) and crash diagnostics to help us fix bugs, per Google's Firebase privacy policy.
  • Firebase Remote Config is used only to let us update the support contact email shown in the app without requiring an app update; it does not collect personal data beyond standard Firebase app-instance identifiers.

Data deletion

Because all game data lives on your device, uninstalling the app deletes it. To stop ad personalization or analytics collection, see your device's ad settings or disable Crashlytics collection in Settings within the app.
