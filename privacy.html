What the app stores

All game data you create — saved games, player names, scores, rules notes, and match history — is stored only on your device (local database) by default. No account or sign-in is required to use any feature.

Optional backup & sync

Settings → "Backup & sync" lets you sign in with Google, entirely optionally, to back up your saved games and match history and sync them across your devices. Signing in sends that same game data to Cloud Firestore (a Google Firebase database), stored under your Google account's unique ID. If you never tap "Sign in with Google", none of your game data ever leaves your device. Signing out stops further sync but does not delete what was already backed up — email the contact below to request deletion of synced data.

Third-party services

  • Google Play Billing processes the optional one-time "Pro" purchase. Payment details are handled entirely by Google Play; this app never sees your payment information.
  • Firebase Authentication issues the anonymous identity every install gets automatically (so the optional sync feature above has somewhere to attach to) and, only if you choose to sign in, your Google account identity (name, email, profile photo) for that same purpose.
  • Cloud Firestore stores your game data only if you've signed in with Google, as described above.
  • Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics collect anonymous usage events (e.g. which screens are opened, whether the Pro purchase completed) and crash diagnostics to help us fix bugs, per Google's Firebase privacy policy.
  • Firebase Remote Config is used only to let us update the support contact email shown in the app without requiring an app update; it does not collect personal data beyond standard Firebase app-instance identifiers.

Scorewright does not show ads and does not use AdMob.

Data deletion

Because all game data lives on your device by default, uninstalling the app deletes it. If you've signed in with Google, uninstalling does not delete the copy already backed up to Cloud Firestore — email the contact below to request that deletion. To stop analytics collection, disable Crashlytics collection in Settings within the app.
